Zero Incident is our first product.

Tech-Yeti is the company. Zero Incident is the first product that demonstrates what our team can design, build, test and present to a client.

First product · featured case study

Zero Incident Safety Training Simulator

A browser-based employee learning system designed around safety training for logistics and warehousing. The current project includes a training hub, three mandatory modules, 360°/3D activities, timed quizzes, progress tracking, trainer guidance and verified certificate handling.

Technology demonstrated

Built as a full application, not only a mockup.

The project uses plain HTML/CSS/browser JavaScript on the frontend, Express on Node.js for the application server, MongoDB through Mongoose, locally bundled Three.js for interactive scenes, server-side sessions, request validation and security middleware.
