# Finalised Zero-Budget Marketing Plan — Tech-Yeti

## Purpose
Promote **Tech-Yeti as a software company** while using Zero Incident as the first concrete product example of our capabilities.

## Zero-cost channels
1. LinkedIn company/team posts: short product clips, service posts and team portfolio posts.
2. University showcases and enterprise events: demonstrate the product and explain company capabilities.
3. Professional networking: direct, respectful outreach to relevant logistics, HSE and technology contacts.
4. Developer / portfolio channels: publish selected technical artefacts and project learning.
5. Referrals: use team, academic and professional networks to introduce interested contacts.

## Website enactment
- The home page positions Tech-Yeti first.
- Services page communicates the company capability set.
- Products page promotes Zero Incident with actual screenshots.
- Team Portfolio page lets prospects inspect each member's contribution.
- Contact page provides a path to enquiry / demo.
