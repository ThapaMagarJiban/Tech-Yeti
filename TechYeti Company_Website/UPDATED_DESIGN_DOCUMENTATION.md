# Updated B2 Website Design Documentation

## Design decision
The B2 website promotes **Tech-Yeti Software Solutions as the business**. The **Zero Incident Safety Training Simulator is presented as Tech-Yeti's first product / featured case study**, not as the company itself.

## Information architecture
Home → About Us → Services → Products → Team Portfolio → Contact.

## B2 alignment
- Website: company-focused promotional site with clear service capabilities.
- Corporate identity: logo, company name, mission and core values.
- Team identity: four named individual portfolios with project contributions.
- Product promotion: Zero Incident shown visually with authentic prototype screenshots and a concise product narrative.
- Conversion: contact/demo journey for prospective clients.

## Visual approach
The existing Tech-Yeti identity is retained: navy/blue foundation, clean sans-serif typography, high-contrast cards, responsive layout and image-led product evidence.
